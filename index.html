The Founder

Built by someone who's been there.

Michael Donahue opened the first Athletic Training Room in 2015 with one goal: bring hands-on sports medicine to athletes who deserve better than a referral chain. No insurance gatekeeping. No protocol shortcuts.

A decade in, the team has grown to seven certified athletic trainers across three Iowa locations. The mission has not changed.

Michael Donahue, ATC, DAT Founder · Owner
The Team

Meet the athletic trainers.

Seven certified athletic trainers serving athletes across central Iowa. Combined decades of experience at the collegiate, professional, and prep levels, plus advanced credentials in dry needling, concussion management, and sport rehabilitation.

Owner · Athletic Trainer

Michael Donahue

A Des Moines native, Michael holds dual bachelor's degrees from the University of Iowa in Athletic Training and Health and Sports Studies, a master's in Higher Education from Iowa State, and a Doctorate in Athletic Training from Temple University. He founded The ATR in 2015 and volunteers at the state, district, and national level to promote the profession.

ATC · DAT · Founder / 2015
Athletic Trainer

Kelly Caldwell

Born and raised in Normal, Illinois, Kelly earned her Master's in Athletic Training from Iowa State University, specializing in women's soccer during her graduate work. She joined The ATR in May 2025 and currently provides athletic training for the Des Moines Menace and Saydel High School, working primarily out of the Des Moines location.

ATC · MS / Joined 2025
Athletic Trainer

Courtney Joint

Courtney grew up in Ottumwa, Iowa, played softball at Southwestern Community College, and earned her BA in Athletic Training from Simpson College in 2016. She holds a Master of Science in Athletic Training with a specialization in Sport Neurology and Concussion from A.T. Still University. She works with the Iowa Barnstormers and Grandview Christian High School.

Born in Indianola, Nealey earned her bachelor's in Athletic Training from the University of Iowa and a master's in Exercise Science and Sport Rehabilitation from Logan University. Megan Brady
